About Verified Glam

Beauty made perfect — on Android and the web

Verified Glam is an AI-powered beauty analysis platform that turns a single selfie into personalized insights — overall beauty scores, facial symmetry breakdowns, color palette suggestions, celebrity look-alike matches, and practical glow-up tips. Our tagline is Pretty in Every Way: we help you understand your features with confidence, not judgment.

What we offer

Verified Glam includes 11 scan types — from core face beauty analysis to seasonal color palettes, attractiveness tests, golden-ratio guides, and fun challenges. Every result is built around your photo: scores and overlays appear directly on your selfie so you can see what the AI detected.

  • Free tier — explore scans with optional ads
  • Pro — full reports, premium features, and an ad-free experience
  • Web app — log in at verifiedglam.com to run scans in your browser
  • Android app — download on Google Play (com.verifiedglam.beauty_scanner)

How it works

  1. Choose a scan type (for example Face Beauty Analysis or Facial Symmetry)
  2. Upload or capture a clear, front-facing selfie
  3. Your photo is sent securely to our servers for AI processing — API keys never live in the app
  4. View personalized scores, text insights, and face overlays on your photo
  5. Save results to your scan history and revisit them anytime

Our approach

Verified Glam is designed for self-discovery and entertainment. AI outputs are generated automatically and may not always be perfectly accurate. They are not medical, dermatological, or professional advice. Features like Celebrity Look-Alike are for fun — they do not verify identity.

We take privacy seriously. Photos are stored in secure cloud storage, processed server-side, and protected by account authentication. Read our Privacy Policy for full details on data collection, retention, and your choices.

Who we serve

Verified Glam is built for beauty-conscious users who want actionable, confidence-oriented feedback — whether you are exploring symmetry, finding your seasonal colors, or following a glow-up routine challenge. We serve users on Android today and on the web for the same core scan experience.
