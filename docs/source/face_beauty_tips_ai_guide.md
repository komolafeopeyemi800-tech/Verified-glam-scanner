## 1. Acne & Breakouts

> **Detected issues may include:** Blackheads, whiteheads, pimples, papules, pustules, cystic acne, nodular acne, comedones

---

### 🔴 High Severity
*Signs: Deep painful bumps, widespread breakouts, cystic or nodular acne covering large areas of the face*

**Tip 1 — Try a raw honey mask overnight**  
A lot of people swear by applying a thin layer of raw honey on their face before bed and leaving it on for 20–30 minutes. Raw honey has natural antibacterial properties that many users say helped calm down their worst breakouts over a few weeks of consistent use. Just rinse off with lukewarm water.

**Tip 2 — Use a gentle cold compress to reduce swelling**  
When those deep painful bumps show up, wrapping an ice cube in a clean cloth and pressing it gently on the spot for a few minutes may help bring down some of the swelling and redness. A lot of YouTubers mention this as their go-to quick fix before filming.

**Tip 3 — Try a diluted apple cider vinegar toner**  
Mix one part apple cider vinegar with three parts water, dab it on with a cotton pad after cleansing, and let it dry. Many creators in the beauty niche say this became part of their daily routine when they were dealing with consistent breakouts. Start slow — once a day and see how your skin responds.

**Tip 4 — Switch to a simple, fragrance-free routine**  
When breakouts are really bad, many people find that stripping back their skincare to just a gentle cleanser and a lightweight moisturizer helped more than adding products. Less products, less irritation. Give your skin a break and see if things improve over two to three weeks.

---

### 🟡 Medium Severity
*Signs: Several pimples or pustules on the face, some blackheads and whiteheads, occasional flare-ups*

**Tip 1 — Spot treat with tea tree oil**  
Dilute a drop of tea tree oil in a small amount of water or a lightweight moisturizer and dab it directly on pimples with a cotton swab. So many beauty creators talk about this as their go-to overnight trick. It may help individual pimples look less red and swollen by morning.

**Tip 2 — Try a green tea toner**  
Brew a cup of green tea, let it cool completely, and use it as a toner on your face. A lot of people in the skincare community have shared that green tea on their face consistently made a visible difference in reducing their mild breakouts over time. You can store it in the fridge for a refreshing feel.

**Tip 3 — Wash your pillowcase every two to three days**  
This one sounds simple but so many people overlook it. Old pillowcases collect oil and bacteria, which some users believe is a big trigger for recurring breakouts, especially on the cheeks and jawline. Many creators say switching to clean cotton pillowcases regularly was a game changer for them.

**Tip 4 — Try a clay or charcoal face mask once a week**  
Clay masks are very popular in the beauty community for drawing out impurities from pores. Using one once a week may help with recurring mild to moderate breakouts. People often talk about feeling their skin looking cleaner and less congested after regular use.

---

### 🟢 Low Severity
*Signs: Occasional pimple, few blackheads or whiteheads, mild congestion*

**Tip 1 — Wash your face with lukewarm water twice a day**  
Hot water can strip your skin and sometimes make things worse. Many people who dealt with occasional breakouts say just switching to lukewarm water and a gentle cleanser morning and night made their skin clearer over time. Simple but effective.

**Tip 2 — Try the rice water rinse**  
This is huge in beauty communities right now. Soak rice in water for 15–20 minutes, strain the water and use it to rinse your face. A lot of Asian beauty creators have been sharing how rice water gave them a clearer, more even complexion over time. It's an old home remedy that is getting a lot of attention again.

**Tip 3 — Avoid touching your face throughout the day**  
Hands carry a lot of bacteria and oils that can transfer to your face and clog pores. Many people who broke out occasionally say being more mindful about not touching their face helped reduce how often new pimples appeared.

**Tip 4 — Apply aloe vera gel as a light moisturizer**  
Pure aloe vera gel straight from the plant or a simple store-bought version is a fan favourite in the natural beauty community. It is lightweight, soothing, and many users say it helped them keep occasional breakouts at bay while keeping their skin hydrated without feeling greasy.

## 2. Hyperpigmentation & Dark Spots

> **Detected issues may include:** Post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (PIH), dark circles, melasma, age spots, sun spots, freckles, dark patches

---

### 🔴 High Severity
*Signs: Widespread dark patches, very prominent dark circles, deep melasma covering large areas, noticeable uneven skin tone*

**Tip 1 — Try a turmeric and honey mask**  
Mix a pinch of turmeric powder with a teaspoon of raw honey and apply it to dark areas for about 15 minutes before rinsing. Turmeric has been used for skin brightening in many cultures for centuries, and a lot of beauty creators on YouTube rave about how this simple mask improved their hyperpigmentation over consistent weeks of use.

**Tip 2 — Use sunscreen every single morning**  
Many people who have seen improvement in dark spots say that wearing SPF daily was the single biggest change they made. Sun exposure can make existing dark spots darker and create new ones. A lot of beauty creators say even on cloudy days they never skip this step.

**Tip 3 — Try the potato slice method**  
Some creators swear by cutting a raw potato into slices and gently rubbing it on dark spots for a few minutes daily. It sounds unusual but it has a big following in natural beauty communities, with many people sharing before-and-after results over a few weeks of regular use.

**Tip 4 — Apply vitamin C from lemon diluted with water**  
Some beauty enthusiasts mix a few drops of fresh lemon juice with equal parts water and apply it to dark areas with a cotton pad. Always test a small area first since some people find lemon too strong for their skin. Those who do tolerate it say they noticed their skin looking brighter after a few weeks of consistent use.

---

### 🟡 Medium Severity
*Signs: Visible dark circles, a few dark spots or marks left from old pimples, moderate uneven tone*

**Tip 1 — Try cucumber slices on dark under-eye areas**  
This is one of the oldest beauty tricks around and it still comes up all the time on YouTube beauty channels. Place cool cucumber slices on your eyes for 10–15 minutes. Many users say regular use gave their under-eye area a noticeably fresher and lighter look over time.

**Tip 2 — Use aloe vera on dark spots daily**  
Apply pure aloe vera gel to dark spots and leave it on as part of your skincare routine. Aloe is gentle enough for regular use and many people in natural beauty communities say it helped gradually fade the marks left behind by old pimples.

**Tip 3 — Try the rice water soak for brightening**  
As mentioned earlier, rice water is a popular home remedy for brightening the skin. Many users in Asian and African beauty communities say regular use helped them achieve a more even skin tone over time. Apply it as a toner after cleansing and let it absorb.

**Tip 4 — Cold spoons or cold eye patches for dark circles**  
Put two spoons in the fridge overnight and press them gently under your eyes in the morning. Many lifestyle and beauty creators say this simple trick helps reduce the appearance of dark circles and puffiness, especially after a rough night of sleep.

---

### 🟢 Low Severity
*Signs: Very mild dark marks, slight unevenness, light freckles or faint post-acne marks*

**Tip 1 — Stay consistent with your cleansing routine**  
A lot of people who have faint dark marks say that simply sticking to a regular morning and evening cleanse helped their skin look more even over time. Clean skin heals and regenerates better.

**Tip 2 — Drink more water daily**  
This sounds basic but the beauty community talks about hydration a lot. Many creators say increasing their water intake to at least 8 glasses a day made their overall complexion look brighter and more glowing within a few weeks.

**Tip 3 — Try rose water as a toner**  
Rose water is a beloved natural ingredient in the beauty world. Spritz it on your face after cleansing or apply with a cotton pad. Many users say it helped even out mild skin tone issues while leaving their skin feeling fresh and soft.

**Tip 4 — Get enough sleep every night**  
Sleep is when your skin repairs itself. A lot of beauty creators say that when they started prioritizing 7–8 hours of quality sleep, their skin tone became noticeably more even and their dark circles looked a lot better. It is free and most powerful.

## 3. Texture Issues & Acne Scars

> **Detected issues may include:** Ice pick scars, boxcar scars, rolling scars, rough skin, bumpy texture, enlarged pores, keloidal scars

---

### 🔴 High Severity
*Signs: Deep pitted scars, widespread uneven texture, visible holes or indentations on the face*

**Tip 1 — Try a rosehip oil massage routine**  
Rosehip oil is very popular in natural beauty communities for fading scars and improving skin texture. Many creators say massaging a few drops into their scarred areas every night before bed gave them visible improvement over a couple of months of consistent use. It soaks in well and is not too heavy.

**Tip 2 — Use a baking soda and water scrub gently once a week**  
Some beauty enthusiasts mix a small amount of baking soda with water to form a paste and use it as a very gentle exfoliant once a week. The idea is to remove dead skin cells and help surface texture improve over time. Go very gently and avoid if your skin feels sensitive.

**Tip 3 — Apply raw honey as a healing mask**  
Raw honey is a popular choice in the natural skincare community for helping the skin repair itself over time. Applying it to scarred areas a few times a week and leaving it on for 20 minutes is a widely shared tip among beauty creators who deal with textured skin.

**Tip 4 — Try aloe vera every night on scarred areas**  
Aloe vera gel is probably the most talked about natural ingredient for skin repair. Many people in the beauty community say applying it nightly to pitted or textured skin helped smooth things out over several weeks of regular use.

---

### 🟡 Medium Severity
*Signs: Some shallow scars or pitting, visible pores, skin feels rough in certain areas*

**Tip 1 — Try an egg white mask for tightening pores**  
Whisk one egg white until slightly foamy, apply a thin layer to your face, let it dry for 10–15 minutes then rinse. Beauty creators have talked about this mask helping to temporarily tighten pores and smooth out mild texture. It is one of those old school home remedies that keeps coming back.

**Tip 2 — Use a gentle sugar scrub once a week**  
Mix a teaspoon of sugar with a little coconut oil and use it as a scrub on rough patches. The sugar helps with gentle physical exfoliation which many users say improved their skin texture noticeably over a few weeks when done once a week.

**Tip 3 — Apply vitamin E oil to shallow scars**  
Vitamin E oil is widely discussed in the beauty community as a go-to for improving the look of minor scars and rough patches. Many creators say massaging it in at night gave them softer and smoother skin over time.

**Tip 4 — Stay consistent with moisturizing**  
Many people with textured skin say that simply staying very consistent with a good moisturizer morning and night helped their skin look and feel smoother over time. Hydrated skin always looks healthier and scars appear less prominent.

---

### 🟢 Low Severity
*Signs: Slightly uneven surface, a few visible pores, mild roughness*

**Tip 1 — Try the ice cube facial massage**  
Wrapping an ice cube in a clean cloth and gently massaging your face with it for a few minutes is a popular beauty hack. Many creators say this tightened their pores and made their skin look smoother instantly. Good for mild texture issues.

**Tip 2 — Use cold green tea as a rinse**  
Brew green tea, cool it in the fridge, and splash it on your face after cleansing. The beauty community loves this for giving the skin a smoother and more refined appearance over regular use.

**Tip 3 — Exfoliate gently once a week**  
Even just a soft washcloth used in gentle circular motions once a week can help remove dead skin buildup that makes pores look bigger and skin look rough. Many beauty creators say this small habit made a big difference to their overall skin texture.

**Tip 4 — Apply coconut oil on very rough patches**  
A small amount of coconut oil on very dry or rough areas is a favourite in natural beauty communities. Many users say it helped smooth out mild texture over time when used consistently as part of their nightly routine.

## 4. Aging & Sagging Skin

> **Detected issues may include:** Wrinkles, fine lines, forehead lines, crow's feet, nasolabial folds, sagging cheeks, jowls, double chin, eye bags, loss of firmness

---

### 🔴 High Severity
*Signs: Deep wrinkles, significant sagging around cheeks and jaw, very pronounced eye bags or double chin*

**Tip 1 — Try a facial massage routine daily**  
A lot of anti-aging beauty creators talk about facial massage as one of the most underrated tools for firming and toning the face. Using your fingertips to massage upward in firm circular motions for 5–10 minutes daily is said to help improve blood flow and reduce the look of sagging over time. Many creators combine this with a facial oil.

**Tip 2 — Do facial yoga exercises every morning**  
Facial yoga is huge in the anti-aging beauty world. Simple exercises like puffing out your cheeks, lifting your eyebrows, and holding for a few seconds are said by many creators to help tone facial muscles over time, similar to how gym exercises tone body muscles. Consistent daily practice is key.

**Tip 3 — Apply egg white as a firming mask**  
The egg white mask comes up a lot in discussions about sagging skin. Many people say applying it to the face and letting it dry creates a temporary tightening effect that over time may help train the skin to look firmer. It is one of those widely shared grandma-style beauty secrets.

**Tip 4 — Try a cold rose water and cucumber compress**  
Soak cotton pads in cold rose water and place cucumber slices on the eye and cheek area. Many beauty creators say this refreshing routine helped their skin look less tired and puffy while giving a mild firming effect over consistent daily use.

---

### 🟡 Medium Severity
*Signs: Moderate wrinkles, some fine lines, early signs of jawline softening, mild under-eye puffiness*

**Tip 1 — Stay consistent with sunscreen use during the day**  
Sun damage is one of the most talked-about contributors to premature aging in the beauty community. Many creators who reversed mild signs of aging say that getting serious about daily SPF was the first and most impactful step they took.

**Tip 2 — Try a castor oil and rosehip oil blend at night**  
Mixing a drop of castor oil with rosehip oil and massaging it into fine lines and loose areas before bed is a popular tip in natural beauty communities. Many users say their skin looked plumper and more hydrated after a few weeks of nightly use.

**Tip 3 — Gua sha facial tool massage**  
Gua sha has become massive in the beauty community. Using a smooth stone tool to massage your face in upward strokes is said to help improve circulation, reduce puffiness, and give the face a more lifted and sculpted look over time. Many creators do this as part of their morning routine.

**Tip 4 — Drink more water and cut back on salty foods**  
A lot of beauty creators talk about how dietary changes visibly impacted their skin's firmness. Drinking enough water and reducing salty processed foods is often credited for helping reduce puffiness and under-eye bags over a couple of weeks.

---

### 🟢 Low Severity
*Signs: A few faint fine lines, very early crow's feet, mild dullness that makes skin look older*

**Tip 1 — Try a banana and honey face mask**  
Mash half a ripe banana with a teaspoon of honey and apply it to your face for 15 minutes. Banana is rich in natural nutrients and this mask is popular in the DIY beauty community for giving the skin a plumper, more youthful glow after regular use.

**Tip 2 — Sleep on your back if you can**  
Many anti-aging beauty creators swear by changing their sleep position to avoid sleep lines becoming permanent creases over time. Sleeping on your back is the most recommended position for keeping the face looking smooth.

**Tip 3 — Always moisturize right after washing your face**  
Applying moisturizer while your face is still slightly damp helps lock in hydration. Many creators say this simple habit made their skin look plumper and fine lines less visible very quickly.

**Tip 4 — Try the aloe vera and vitamin E nightly routine**  
Mixing a small amount of aloe vera gel with a drop of vitamin E oil and applying it before bed is a widely loved natural anti-aging tip. Many beauty creators say it helped their skin look fresher and more hydrated in the mornings.

## 5. Sensitivity & Redness

> **Detected issues may include:** Reactive skin, rosacea-like redness, flushing, burning sensation, facial irritation, broken capillaries, stinging after product use

---

### 🔴 High Severity
*Signs: Persistent widespread redness, constant burning or stinging feeling, visible broken capillaries, skin reacts strongly to almost everything*

**Tip 1 — Strip your routine back to bare minimum**  
Many people with very reactive skin say the best thing they ever did was stop using multiple products and go back to just a gentle cleanser and a plain soothing moisturizer. Less is more when your skin is very sensitive. Give it at least two to four weeks to calm down.

**Tip 2 — Apply pure aloe vera gel straight from the plant**  
Aloe vera is the most talked-about natural soother in the beauty community for irritated and red skin. Many creators with sensitive skin say keeping an aloe plant at home and applying the gel directly has been more effective than many products they spent money on.

**Tip 3 — Use oat milk or colloidal oatmeal as a rinse**  
Blending oats with water, straining the liquid, and using it to rinse your face is a natural remedy that beauty creators with very reactive skin often recommend. Oat milk is gentle and soothing and many users say it helped calm persistent redness over time.

**Tip 4 — Avoid hot showers and steam on your face**  
Many people with sensitive and red skin say that switching to cooler water when washing their face and avoiding steam made a noticeable difference in how red and reactive their skin was on a day-to-day basis.

---

### 🟡 Medium Severity
*Signs: Redness that comes and goes, face flushes easily, occasionally stings after product use*

**Tip 1 — Try a cooled chamomile tea compress**  
Brew chamomile tea, let it cool completely, soak a clean cloth in it and press gently on red areas. Chamomile is widely loved in natural beauty communities for its calming properties. Many users say this helped take down mild redness and soothe irritation within minutes.

**Tip 2 — Switch to fragrance-free products**  
A really common tip from creators who reduced their facial redness was cutting out all fragranced skincare products. Fragrance is one of the most talked-about triggers for skin reactions and many people saw improvement quickly after making this swap.

**Tip 3 — Apply cold rose water mist throughout the day**  
Keeping rose water in a small spray bottle in the fridge and spritzing it on your face when redness or heat flares up is a popular natural beauty hack. Many creators with flushing-prone skin say it became a non-negotiable part of their day.

**Tip 4 — Try a green tea compress on red areas**  
Cool green tea applied with a cotton pad or clean cloth on flushed areas is a widely shared tip in beauty communities for calming redness. Green tea is consistently mentioned by creators as one of the best natural ingredients for reactive skin.

---

### 🟢 Low Severity
*Signs: Occasional mild redness, skin gets a little pink after washing, minor sensitivity*

**Tip 1 — Always use lukewarm water to wash your face**  
Very hot or very cold water can trigger mild redness in sensitive skin. Most beauty creators with sensitive skin recommend lukewarm water as the sweet spot for cleansing without triggering a reaction.

**Tip 2 — Pat your face dry instead of rubbing**  
Rubbing with a towel can irritate mild sensitivity. Gently patting dry with a clean soft cloth is a widely recommended swap in the beauty community that many people say reduced their everyday redness noticeably.

**Tip 3 — Apply a thin layer of aloe vera before moisturizer**  
Using aloe vera as a base layer before your regular moisturizer is a tip shared widely among creators with occasionally sensitive skin. Many say it added a buffer that kept their skin feeling calmer throughout the day.

**Tip 4 — Stay away from spicy food on days your skin is flaring**  
Many people with mild skin sensitivity notice it gets worse when they eat very spicy food. Beauty creators often mention this connection and recommend being mindful of diet when your face is going through a reactive phase.

## 6. Oily Skin & Enlarged Pores

> **Detected issues may include:** Excess shine, greasy appearance, enlarged or clogged pores, blackheads due to oiliness, sebum buildup, acne-prone oily skin

---

### 🔴 High Severity
*Signs: Face is shiny and greasy within an hour of washing, very large visible pores, constant oil buildup throughout the day*

**Tip 1 — Try a clay mask two to three times a week**  
Clay masks are one of the most consistently recommended natural solutions for oily skin in the beauty community. Many creators say using a clay or kaolin mask several times a week was the thing that finally helped them control their oil production and visibly reduce the size of their pores.

**Tip 2 — Use blotting papers throughout the day**  
This is a super popular tip among creators with very oily skin. Blotting papers absorb excess oil without disturbing your routine. Many beauty creators keep them in their bag and say using them regularly throughout the day made a big difference in how their skin looked and felt.

**Tip 3 — Try washing with a gentle cleanser twice a day — not more**  
A lot of people with very oily skin think washing more often will help but many beauty creators say over-washing actually makes your skin produce even more oil. Sticking to twice a day with a gentle cleanser is consistently recommended as the better approach.

**Tip 4 — Apply a thin layer of honey as a mask once a week**  
Raw honey is naturally antibacterial and many people in the beauty community say applying it as a mask once a week helped reduce how oily their skin was, while also keeping their skin soft and clear.

---

### 🟡 Medium Severity
*Signs: Gets oily by midday, some visible pores mainly in T-zone, occasional oil-related breakouts*

**Tip 1 — Try the rice water toner after cleansing**  
Rice water is very popular for controlling shine. Many creators say using it as a toner after their morning cleanse helped mattify their skin and keep oiliness under control throughout the day.

**Tip 2 — Apply witch hazel as a natural toner**  
Witch hazel is one of the most talked-about natural astringents in the beauty community. Many people with moderately oily skin say using it as a toner after cleansing helped reduce shine and tighten the look of their pores noticeably over consistent use.

**Tip 3 — Use tomato pulp as a quick face mask**  
Applying fresh tomato pulp to the face and leaving it for 10 minutes is a home remedy popular in many beauty communities. Tomatoes are said to help balance the skin's natural oil production and many users report their skin looking less shiny after regular use.

**Tip 4 — Change your pillowcase frequently**  
Oil from your face transfers to your pillowcase overnight and comes right back onto your skin. Many creators with oily skin say changing their pillowcase every two to three days was one of the most surprisingly effective steps they took.

---

### 🟢 Low Severity
*Signs: Slightly oily T-zone, occasional shine by end of day, pores moderately visible*

**Tip 1 — Try ending your morning cleanse with a cool water splash**  
Finishing with cool water is a widely shared tip for mildly oily skin. Many beauty creators say this simple step helped keep shine at bay a little longer throughout the day.

**Tip 2 — Use aloe vera gel as your daily moisturizer**  
For those with mildly oily skin, heavy moisturizers can worsen shine. Many creators recommend pure aloe vera gel as a lightweight hydrating option that does not clog pores or add to oiliness.

**Tip 3 — Try a light egg white mask once a week**  
Egg white masks are popular for their pore-tightening effect. Many beauty enthusiasts with mild oiliness say doing this once a week helped reduce how much their pores contributed to shine.

**Tip 4 — Avoid touching your face during the day**  
Hands transfer oils from everything you touch directly onto your face. Many creators say being mindful of this one habit reduced their mild shine and breakouts significantly over time.

**Tip 4 — Use oat milk as a face rinse instead of plain water**  
Blending oats with water, straining it, and using it as a facial rinse is a popular natural remedy for extremely dry skin. Many users in the beauty community say the oat milk rinse left their skin feeling moisturized and soothed rather than tight after washing.

---

Many beauty creators with dry skin say this one change made their moisturizer work much better. Rather than waiting until your face is fully dry after washing, applying while still slightly damp locks in more moisture and keeps skin hydrated longer.

**Tip 2 — Try a banana and milk face mask**  
Mash a ripe banana with a tablespoon of milk and apply for 15 minutes. This is a widely loved DIY beauty treatment for dull and dry skin. Many creators say their skin looked noticeably more glowing and soft after trying this a few times a week.

**Tip 3 — Use aloe vera and rose water together as a daily toner**  
Mix equal parts aloe vera gel and rose water, apply after cleansing. This is a popular natural duo in the beauty community that many users say kept their skin hydrated and gave it a healthy glow throughout the day.

---

### 🟢 Low Severity

**Tip 2 — Try the rice water rinse for added glow**  
Rice water is not just for brightening — many users also report it helped their slightly dry skin feel more nourished and look more glowing after regular use as a simple daily toner.

**Tip 3 — Add a slice of cucumber to your water**  
Cucumber-infused water is a popular wellness and beauty trick. Many creators say staying well hydrated with cucumber water made their skin look plumper and less dull over a couple of weeks.

**Tip 4 — Never skip moisturizer even if your skin feels okay**  
Many beauty creators say the biggest mistake they made with mildly dry skin was assuming it was fine and skipping moisturizer. Consistency with a lightweight moisturizer morning and night is always recommended to keep mild dryness from getting worse.

## 8. Uneven Skin Tone & Rosacea

---

### 🔴 High Severity
*Signs: Heavily blotchy skin, very noticeable redness or skin tone inconsistency across the face, patches of discoloration*

**Tip 1 — Try a turmeric and yogurt brightening mask**  
Mix a small pinch of turmeric with plain yogurt and apply to the face for 10–15 minutes. Turmeric is deeply rooted in natural beauty traditions across the world and many creators share it as their go-to remedy for evening out skin tone over regular use. The lactic acid in yogurt is also widely credited for helping fade uneven pigmentation.

**Tip 2 — Apply aloe vera gel consistently twice a day**  
Aloe vera is one of the most widely recommended natural treatments for uneven skin tone in the beauty community. Many creators say making it part of their twice-daily routine led to visibly more even and balanced skin after a few weeks.

**Tip 3 — Use cold green tea compresses on blotchy areas**  
Cooled green tea applied with a cotton pad on uneven or red patches is a widely shared beauty tip. Many users say regular application helped reduce the patchiness of their skin tone over time.

**Tip 4 — Protect your face from the sun every day**  
Sun exposure is one of the most talked-about reasons skin tone becomes uneven. Many creators with heavily uneven skin say that being diligent about using SPF and wearing a hat outdoors made a very visible difference over several weeks.

---

### 🟡 Medium Severity
*Signs: Some patches of redness or darker areas, skin tone varies across the face, mild blotchiness*

**Tip 1 — Try apple cider vinegar diluted as a toner**  
Mix one part ACV with three parts water and apply after cleansing with a cotton pad. This is a well-known natural beauty tip in online communities for people dealing with uneven skin tone. Many users say it helped balance out mild patchiness over consistent daily use.

**Tip 2 — Use a tomato and lemon juice face rinse**  
Mix a little fresh tomato juice with a few drops of diluted lemon juice and apply to uneven areas. Many beauty creators share this as a brightening and evening home treatment. Always test a small area first to see how your skin responds.

**Tip 3 — Try a honey and oat scrub once a week**  
Mix oats with honey to form a gentle paste and use it as a light weekly exfoliant. Many users with moderate uneven tone say this helped smooth out patchiness over time by gently removing dead skin and allowing more even skin to show through.

**Tip 4 — Keep your stress levels in check**  
Many beauty creators talk about how stress visibly affected their skin tone and caused flare-ups of redness and patchiness. Practices like getting enough sleep, taking walks, and reducing screen time before bed are frequently mentioned as things that helped their skin balance out over time.

---

### 🟢 Low Severity
*Signs: Slightly uneven here and there, skin looks a little patchy under certain lighting, minimal redness*

**Tip 1 — Try the potato juice natural brightener**
